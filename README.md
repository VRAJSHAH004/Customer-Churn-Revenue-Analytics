# Customer Churn & Transaction Analytics using SQL and Python

## Project Overview

This project analyzes customer churn and revenue using SQL and Python to identify customer behavior patterns, churn drivers, and revenue-generating customer segments. The analysis provides business insights and recommendations to improve customer retention and maximize long-term revenue.

An AI-generated business report was also created using Google Gemini AI and exported as a professional PDF.

---

## Business Problem

Customer churn directly impacts business revenue and long-term growth. The company needs to understand:

- Why customers are leaving
- Which customers are leaving
- Which customers generate the most revenue
- Which customer segments are most valuable
- Which business areas require improvement

---

## Project Goal

Analyze customer behavior, churn, and revenue using SQL and Python to generate actionable business insights that support customer retention strategies.

## Key Findings

- Month-to-Month customers have the highest churn.
- Customers within their first 12 months are most likely to churn.
- Two-Year contract customers generate the highest lifetime revenue.
- Fiber Optic customers generate high revenue but also experience higher churn.
- Customer tenure has the strongest relationship with lifetime revenue.

---

## Business Recommendations

- Encourage customers to switch to long-term contracts.
- Improve onboarding during the first year.
- Enhance Fiber Optic service quality.
- Promote automatic payment methods.
- Reward loyal long-term customers.
- Continuously monitor customer churn KPIs.
